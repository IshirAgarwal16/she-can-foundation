Common Questions

You might be wondering...

Do I need any specific skills or experience to volunteer? +
Not at all. We welcome volunteers from all backgrounds. Whether you're a teacher, a student, a professional, or a homemaker — there's a role for you. We provide full training and will match you to opportunities that suit your strengths.
How much time do I need to commit? +
We have opportunities starting from just 2 hours per month. We understand that life is busy, and we design our volunteer roles to be flexible. You can increase your involvement over time as you get comfortable.
Can I volunteer remotely? +
Yes! Many of our roles — including digital literacy instruction, content creation, mentorship calls, and administrative support — can be done entirely online. We have volunteers contributing from across the country and internationally.
How is my donation used? +
We publish annual impact reports showing exactly how funds are allocated. Typically, 82% goes directly to programs, 12% to operations, and 6% to fundraising. We are committed to full financial transparency.
Can my company partner with She Can Foundation? +
Absolutely. We have corporate partnership programs that include employee volunteering, program sponsorship, skills-based volunteering, and employment pathways for our graduates. Reach out via the form above and mention "corporate partnership."
