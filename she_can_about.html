Our Story

We believe every woman already has what it takes.

She Can Foundation exists to remove the obstacles — not to create the strength. That's already there.

Our Mission

Removing barriers, not building ceilings.

Founded in 2015 by a group of women who had each faced their own version of "you can't," She Can Foundation was built on a radical idea: that the problem was never the women — it was the systems around them.

We work in communities where women face the steepest climbs — limited access to education, economic exclusion, and social barriers that tell them to stay small. We show up with resources, mentorship, and an unshakeable belief in their potential.

Our programs are designed without judgment, without unrealistic expectations, and with deep respect for each woman's unique journey.

What We Stand For

Our core values.

The principles that guide every decision we make.

01
Dignity First
Every woman who walks through our doors is treated with complete respect. No judgment, no conditions, no hierarchy of worthiness.
02
Radical Inclusion
Age, background, education level, language — none of these are barriers to participation. We meet women exactly where they are.
03
Practical Impact
We measure success in real outcomes: income earned, businesses started, children kept in school, confidence rebuilt.
04
Community Ownership
We don't parachute in with solutions. We build alongside communities so programs outlast our presence.
05
Transparency
Every rupee donated is accounted for. We publish our impact reports and financials because trust is earned, not assumed.
06
Joy in the Journey
Transformation doesn't have to be grim. We celebrate every milestone, every small win, every brave first step.
Our Journey

Nine years of showing up.

2015
The Beginning
Founded by five women in a single room with a shared conviction: that women in their communities deserved better opportunities and a safe space to grow.
🌱
📚
2017
First Literacy Program
Launched our flagship literacy and numeracy program, reaching 200 women in our first cohort. 94% completed the full course.
2019
Expanding to 10 Communities
Grew from 1 to 10 active communities. Introduced the mentorship program, pairing graduates with newer participants.
🌍
💻
2021
Digital Skills Initiative
Launched digital literacy training during the pandemic, proving that technology access is a right, not a privilege.
2024
12,000+ Women Served
Crossed a milestone we once only dreamed of. Now operating in 48 communities across 6 states with 320+ active volunteers.
